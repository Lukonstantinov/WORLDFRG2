Manufactured goods 23 chain goods

Finished goods made in cities from imported raws — they have no per-cell belt; the shared apply_manufacturing pass produces them at populous hubs that hold the inputs (output ∝ labor ∝ population). Values below are base_value in grain-equivalent.

Viability check ✔ — all 23 are viable. Coats of arms — houses & guilds

Arms are deterministic: a holder's tinctures, shield shape, division, ordinary and charge are all derived from its name hash, so a given house always shows the same arms. Below is the complete component library — every atomic element the generator can combine. A private house draws the rich dynastic system (shape + fur + division + ordinary + figurative charge); a civic guild draws the simpler geometric mark.

How many arms are possible? Houses combine 8 fields × 5 shapes × 3 fur states × 11 divisions × 10 ordinaries × 16 charges (plus the contrasting metal/colour picks per the rule of tincture) — tens of thousands of distinct blazons. Guilds combine 8 fields × 4 divisions × 6 charges. Every combination renders as crisp, tintable SVG.

The Bank — a chartered balance-sheet institution

🏦 Bank "Banco di <City>"

A great merchant-banking house's chartered institution with a real balance sheet. Chartering opens in the "age of banking" (year 20) and requires a great fortune; the charter/establishment fee is paid to the seat city's treasury. Counting-house branches follow the owner's trade offices, extending the home coin's reach and letting its bank-notes shave freight cost in branch cities. A bank that runs its reserve ratio too thin is vulnerable to a run; a failed bank can ignite a regional crash (contagion).

Assets
specie reserves + loans outstanding + real_estate (branches / foreclosed property) + stakes (equity in manufactories)
Liabilities
deposits (capital placed by other houses) + notes_issued (bank-notes in circulation)
Equity
assets − liabilities (tracked yearly for the panel's history charts)
Charter opens
year 20 · fee → seat-city treasury
Interest
1%/mo on liquid capital, capped at 20 (banker houses) — a flat early boost, not uncapped compounding
Credit reach
×1.6 financing beyond cash (banker archetype trades on credit)
