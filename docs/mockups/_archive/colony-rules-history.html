B · The historical models

Phoenician
Emporion → apoikia. Merchant city-states (Tyre, Sidon) plant coastal trading posts sited by CARGO — tin, silver, purple — a day's sail apart. Founded by the mother-city + a leading family, seeded small, tied by cult & trade, not conquest. Gadir/Utica/Carthage grew into cities.
Greek
Apoikia. Population pressure & land hunger. An oikist leads settlers; the colony is politically independent from birth but keeps the mother-city's cult. Syracuse, Massalia.
Roman
Colonia. State-directed, strategic. Veteran/citizen settlement on conquered land, road-linked, a garrison that Romanizes. Ostia is Rome's port satellite. Politically = Rome; never secedes.
Portuguese
Feitoria. Crown / chartered-company financed fortified trading post for one good (gold, pepper). Very long ocean reach. Some (Goa, Luanda) became full colonies.

New rule 1 — Outpost → Colony (emporion → city)

A house outpost that grows past a pop/trade threshold and attracts settlers graduates to a full colony (Gadir, Utica). Lifts today's hard pop cap for outposts that thrive. The founding house keeps its stake.

New rule 2 — Colonies spawn colonies

An independent colony that becomes a metropolis in its own right becomes an eligible founder — Carthage planted Cartagena, Ibiza. Recursion of the whole system.

New rule 3 — Founding dynasty (the Magonids)

The founding house keeps a controlling office in the colony from birth (council_house = founder house). This is the "default control by the trade family that built it."

New rule 4 — Metropolis decline emancipates (Tyre falls)

When Nebuchadnezzar besieged Tyre, Carthage rose. If the mother city is sacked / abandoned / shrinks below a floor, its colonies auto-emancipate.

D · Independence, the Carthaginian way

When a colony secedes — triggered by any of: it grows richer/bigger than its mother (wealth or pop ratio > k, like Carthage vs a fallen Tyre); it sits far from the metropolis (distance loosens the leash); or the metropolis declines (rule 4).

Peaceful drift (default)
Control → the founding house. The trade dynasty that built the colony (the Magonids of Carthage) takes it as a free city. It gets its own settlement window as an independent hub; council_house stays the founder house.
War of independence
The mother-city's house is expelled; a new revolutionary trade family is spawned locally to lead the freed city (a fresh house at that hub). This is the "new trade family appears" case.
