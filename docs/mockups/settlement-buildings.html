15 building ideas — ranked best → worst

#BuildingEffect on settlement / house / guild
1💧 Aqueduct / CisternRaises population carrying capacity + cuts disease. City grows larger; more workers for estates/manufactories.
2🏦 Counting-house (named bank)Shows the bank's name; gives the bank a deposit/lending bonus + the city a credit-availability boost. House gains a branch.
3⚓ Harbour / MoleRaises sea-trade throughput + lowers shipping loss for resident houses. Maritime cities boom.
4⚜️ Merchant quarter (Fondaco)Exclusive district granted to one family/nation → trade monopoly bonus there, rent to the city, prestige to the house. Rivals pay a toll.
5🎓 University / StudioFaster manufactory quality gains + resists technique theft. Guilds prosper.
6🏰 Walls / FortificationsWar defense: cuts blockade/levy damage in economic wars; stabilises morale.
7⛲ Market square / LoggiaLowers local price spread + raises trade volume (more buyers/sellers meet). City tax take rises.
8⚖️ Mint houseBoosts coin trust/fineness ceiling + seigniorage to the council. Strengthens the city's currency.
9⛪ Cathedral / TempleBig prestige + stability; draws pilgrims (a steady demand sink). Festivals hit harder.
10🏥 Lazaretto / HospitalShortens plague lock-ups (the contract force-majeure) + reduces famine mortality.
11🛟 Drydock / ArsenalBuilds bigger/cheaper ships for the resident fleet house (Venetian Arsenal).
12🧵 Guild factory hallA guild's own works hub: concentrates manufacture + raises guild output/quality.
13🛣 Road / Bridge worksLowers inland freight on routes through the city (a land Guildhall).
14🏚 Almshouse / Granary doleBuffers famine (slows population loss) but is a civic money sink.
15🗼 Clock tower / MonumentPure prestige/flavour; minor stability. Lowest functional value.
