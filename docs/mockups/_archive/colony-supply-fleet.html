What changed (mechanics)

  • Food reaches colonies. Root cause: the old lifeline gated delivery on the metropolis treasury (which drained to 0 → 0 food) and looked for a supplier of only the first food good. Now a colony draws real grain from a designated food source (biggest reachable producer) via a dedicated fleet; freight is best-effort so a thin treasury no longer cuts the food off.
  • Dedicated supply ships. Each colony keeps supply_ships on the grain run (capacity {SUPPLY_SHIP_CAPACITY}=900/mo each). When short, the metropolis invests in more ships (paid by backers: city treasury → bank → house), up to 12 — steady supply.
  • Sufficient source. A source only ships grain above a {SOURCE_BUFFER_DAYS}=20-day self-buffer × a spare fraction, so it never starves itself — a colony is only fed from a genuinely sufficient source.
  • Grain colonies (Greek Crimea). A big city in sustained food shortage plants a farming colony (self-funded, no bank needed) on the most fertile reachable site; its surplus flows back through the market.
  • No mainland lines. The colony↔metropolis supply lanes were removed from the map.
  • Clickable colonies. The map hit-tests live campaign hubs, so a click on a colony/outpost selects it and opens the Colonial Office detail.
