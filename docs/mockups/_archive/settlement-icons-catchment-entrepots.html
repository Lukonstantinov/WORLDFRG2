2 · Goods-collection radius (bounded catchment)

Before — unbounded nearest-hub Voronoi

After — fixed radius by size (50 km → 70 km)

Scale reality check: at the default 3,600-wide world, 1 cell ≈ 11.1 km, so 50 km ≈ 4.5 cells and 70 km ≈ 6.3 cells. That is finer than the current coarse catchment grid (f = grid_w/220 ≈ 16 cells ≈ 178 km). Implementation must gather at a bounded fine-resolution window around each hub, not the coarse Voronoi.

Balance risk: tiny radii mean most goods belts are collected by no one → hub production drops sharply and trade thins. Recommended: radius lerps 50→70 km by population/tier, and we re-run cargo test --lib simulate_decades_reports_dynamics -- --nocapture to confirm the economy still lives. This may need a companion tweak (e.g. per-cell yield up) to keep totals sane.

3 · Satellites must sit next to the metropolis

Before — satellite pulled from the far colonizable pool

After — dedicated near-metro site scan

maybe_found_satellite keeps SATELLITE_REACH_FRAC=0.06 but the site source is the problem: it draws from self.colonizable, whose sites are buffered ≥1,600 km from every city. Fix: give satellites their own tight near-metro candidate scan (a few cells out, ignoring the colony buffer), OR shrink the buffer so near sites exist. Recommend the former so colonies stay far while satellites hug home.

4 · Dynamic entrepôts & trade hubs (campaign-only) — brainstorm

Today: "emporium / trade hub / stars" are decided statically in compute_political + compute_economy from geography (throughput = production × centrality) — a frozen worldgen preview. They do not react to the living campaign, even though the tick already moves real cargo and even charges a transit toll on goods passing THROUGH a city.

Goal: a hub earns "trade hub" / "entrepôt" status during the campaign from the trade that actually flows through it — and can lose it when trade shifts (Bruges silts up, Antwerp rises).

Signal
  • Transit volume = cargo a hub's merchants carry that neither originates nor ends there (the Venice signal). Already partly tracked via toll hubs / flow_year.
  • New per-hub yearly accumulator transit_year.
Classify (yearly)
  • Trade hub (class 1): transit in world top ~20%, or transit > k× local production.
  • Entrepôt (class 2): sea-accessible + top few by transit + transit > local consumption.
Hysteresis
  • Gain status after N=3 straight years above threshold.
  • Lose it after M=3 years below. Stops flicker; makes rise/fall a story.

Emergence timeline (one hub)

Scope guard: only runs while campaign.active. Pre-campaign, the Political step keeps its static preview. New field hub_class:u8 on CampaignHubBrief → OverlayManager draws a blue ring (trade hub) or red ring (entrepôt) around the population dot — layered on top of the part-1 marker, so both "how big" and "how commercial" read together.

Open questions for you

  • Catchment totals: OK for hub production to drop with the small radius (more realistic, thinner trade), or should per-cell yield rise to keep total output roughly constant?
  • Radius gradient: lerp 50→70 km smoothly by population, or hard steps (outpost 50 / village 55 / town 60 / city 70)?
  • Entrepôt rings vs replacing markers: ring around the pop dot (shown here) — or a distinct shape as today (red triangle / gold square)?
  • Should the static Political-step preview be removed once campaign entrepôts exist, or kept as a "geographic potential" pre-campaign view?
