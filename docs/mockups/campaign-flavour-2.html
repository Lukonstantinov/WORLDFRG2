Campaign flavour II — the rest of the packs

Route-borne epidemics, dynastic marriages, craft guilds, and the lighter set (fashion, civic wonders, piracy, diaspora). Each extends machinery that already existed, stays bounded, and reads through the chronicle — the dynamics test stays green.

Epidemics reuse the existing plague (population cull + contract quarantine) and add contagion that hops city-to-city along the trade lanes. Marriages reuse house feuds (rivals). Guild strikes reuse the production-shock path (shared with embargo). Piracy reuses fleet-loss. Wonders + fashion + diaspora are new but tiny.

Each pack & its bounded effect

PackWhat happensBounded effect
☠ Epidemics Plague spreads city-to-city along live trade lanes from an outbreak focus. Milder cull than the origin (0.08), ≤2 new foci/tick, hard cap ~25% of cities locked → burns out & recovers.
💍 Marriages A prominent house weds another; a broken match rekindles the feud. Ends a feud (rivals), capped dowry only moves wealth between houses, small prestige.
🏛 Craft guilds City guilds master a good, occasionally strike, and raise a guildhall. Capped quality lift; a strike is a short, capped manufacture dent; guildhall = +small stability.
👗 Fashion A luxury becomes the rage for a couple of years, then fades. Capped (≤1.4×) transient demand lift on one good → its price rises, then relaxes.
🗿 Wonders A prosperous city raises a lighthouse → market hall → cathedral. +small stability, one-time per tier; purely additive.
🏴 Piracy Corsairs seize a merchant house's galley. −1 sea-fleet asset (floored at 0); reuses the existing fleet-loss path.
🧭 Diaspora A house with a far-flung office founds a resident quarter (fondaco). +small standing influence at the host city; reuses the office/influence system.
Verification: the standing dynamics run stays bounded & recovers after every pack — wealth in the same range, house turnover healthy, no famine collapse; 32/32 tick tests pass and determinism holds. The epidemic tuning (contagion magnitude + the lock cap) is the one dial to raise for Black-Death severity.
