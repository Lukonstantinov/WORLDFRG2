4 · Migration made visible

  • Economic drift (new, yearly): within a trade region, people leave poor/hungry cities (opportunity < 0.55) for the most thriving reachable one — capped at 2%/yr, dynamics-bounded.
  • Arrows now drawn for ALL migration — economic drift + polis-sponsored waves both push a fading "refugee road" arrow (was: only city deaths drew arrows, so migration looked broken).
  • Each wave carries its culture into the destination's minority quarters (§3), and writes a Timeline beat: "320 people leave Askar seeking work in Vex Port."
  • Toggle: Toolbar ▸ Overlays ▸ 🚶 Migrations (already present, on by default).

5 · Several houses per city — the real root fix

Root cause found: maybe_found_house blocked a new family wherever the strongest incumbent had wealth > 8.0 — but houses run at 100 000+, so every city with an established house was permanently locked out, collapsing the whole world to one dynasty over a century. Fix: dropped the wealth lockout; a hub now hosts up to 3 competing houses (rich) / 2 (small) by a size cap, no matter how rich the incumbents are. Also relaxed the office-open gate 0.5→0.3× so houses plant second-tier offices too.
Note: the 30-hub dynamics test is trade-starved (trade_wealth ≤ 0.05 everywhere → founding filtered before the gate), so it can't demonstrate plurality; the fix is structural for real 681-city campaigns.

6 · Colonies grow up · dead cities come back

  • Colony independence: gate relaxed 70y / city-stage (40k) → 50y / town-stage (15k). On independence the colony becomes a full free city (colony_kind→0) — war of independence if the metropolis still stands, else peacefully.
  • Ruin revival: a ruin dead ≥15 y with a thriving food-secure city nearby in its trade region is resettled — pioneers refound a town at pop 500, and its buried productive potential (base_per_capita, preserved at death) refills trade next tick. The living settlement icon returns (with a gold "new" star) and a Timeline beat: "Pioneers resettle the ruins of Askar — the town lives again." Trickle-capped (≤2/yr).
