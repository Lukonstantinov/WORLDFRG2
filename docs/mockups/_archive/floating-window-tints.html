Floating windows — distinct tints + drag-to-move

Every floating sub-window now (1) carries a faint distinct tint so you can tell them apart at a glance, and (2) is draggable by its title bar (grab ⠿, drop anywhere; the ✕/tabs still click normally). Tints are defined in src/ui/useFloatingWindow.ts (PANEL_TINTS) and mirrored below.

  • Drag handle = the title bar (cursor: move); children marked data-no-drag (close, tabs) don't start a drag.
  • First drag seeds from the window's live position so it doesn't jump; windows can't be dragged off the top-left edge.
  • Applied to: Coin/Credit, Speculation, Houses, Goods market, Richest Cities, World News, Warehouses, Futures.
