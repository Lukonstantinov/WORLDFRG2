Trade & World Dynamics 2.0

"See what happens in the world and why" — the Living Map (cities born ✦ and dying †), the Trade Heat lens, and the World Atlas · brainstorm + design + implementation report

0 · The brief, and what the world could already do

The ask: watch trade concentrate into regions, watch settlements appear and cities die (with a cross), and navigate stats/graphs that explain why. The audit found the simulation was closer than expected — and blocked in three places:

CapabilityBefore 2.0
Settlements appearingOnly as chartered COLONIES (joint-stock ventures from year 50). No organic growth — a thriving free city never spawned a daughter town.
Cities dyingOnly colonies could collapse — and a latent bug meant even those resurrected: the population floor (max(founding_pop·0.10)) revived every "dead" hub at 10% strength the next tick, so the † cross never survived.
Trade regionsflow_year recorded every city-pair's yearly volume and drew trunk LINES — but nothing showed where trade concentrates, and no per-city throughput stat existed at all.
Graphs / navigable statsPer-city history charts existed in the settlement window; the WORLD had only a price-index sparkline. No census, no lifecycle timeline, no world graphs.

1 · The questions (asked; channel failed) & the answers taken

How brutal should organic city death be? → Moderate + migration. Famine-driven only: a town shrunk to the famine floor and starving for 8 straight years is abandoned; survivors take the road. Poverty alone never kills (see §4 — the first draft that allowed it emptied 29 of 30 test towns).
How should new settlements appear? → Organic + colonial. Chartered colonies stay; from year 25 a thriving city bursting past 1.55× its founding size swarms — 7% of its people found an independent town on fertile free land nearby.
How to show where trade concentrates? → Heat overlay first. Additive glow per city ∝ its yearly throughput; named "trade basins" proposed for phase 2.
Which Atlas tabs? → Overview graphs + Cities census + Timeline (Regions drill-down deferred to phase 2).

2 · The Living Map SHIPPED

One map, three new signals. 🔥 Trade Heat (toggle in the HUD, Toolbar or the Atlas): additive teal→gold→crimson glows per city, radius and colour ∝ its share of the busiest market's yearly throughput — overlapping basins genuinely glow, so "the regions where trade happens a lot" read instantly. ✦ Founding star: a settlement founded this campaign wears gold for its first 15 years. † Ruin cross: an abandoned city keeps a parchment-haloed cross forever (and — bug fixed — actually stays dead).

4 · Tuning story — how the standing dynamics test shaped the rules

The first draft let misery (mood < 0.18) qualify a town for death. The standing 50-year dynamics run answered immediately:

yr 15: … towns 20 (+0/−10) … yr 25: … towns 1 (+0/−29) … ← the world ground itself empty

Instrumenting the digest showed why: the harsh 6-good toy world lives in universal chronic famine (hungry 30 · thriving 0) — any purely local rule kills everyone. The fix is the pull factor: abandonment is migration, so it requires a haven — a fed, living town to walk to. If everywhere is hungry, people endure where they are:

yr 50: houses 2↑/8✝ banks 1 coins 10 … towns 30 (+0/−0) hungry 30 thriving 0 … over 50y: wealth ∈ [-4.6, 508981.0] · houses turn over · all asserts green

The mechanics themselves are proven by two new deterministic tests: thriving_city_swarms_a_daughter_town (site consumed, settlers leave, founding chronicled) and famine_town_is_abandoned_and_stays_dead (survivors reach the haven, the ruin survives the population floor — the resurrect bug's regression test). The digest now permanently reports towns (+F/−A) hungry N thriving M.
