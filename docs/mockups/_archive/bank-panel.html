Toggle 🏦 Banks in Toolbar → Overlays. Each live bank shows a gold disc ringed in its owning house's colour at its seat; clicking one opens the Bank panel focused on that bank. Failed banks are hidden.

How it works (explainer tab)

Loan interest — lends to houses (trade), guilds (factories & civic works) and city treasuries (public works) at 1.2%/mo. Interest is profit; principal repayment retires the notes that funded the loan (the bug that destroyed principal — and every bank — is fixed).

Equity stakes — buys a 25% share of a manufactory and draws that share of its income as a dividend (grows net worth from real production).

Deposits & notes — wealthy houses deposit capital; the bank issues notes up to 3× reserves. Keep the reserve ratio above 22% or a crash can trigger a fatal run.
