⏳ Era scrubber (Atlas ▸ Overview)

⏳ Year 187return to present

A gold year slider under the Overview tiles. Dragging into the past makes the MAP itself time-travel: settlement markers re-tier to that year's populations, dead cities show † only if they had already fallen, founding stars ✦ only if the town was still young, and the Trade Heat glows with that year's volumes. Migration arrows hide while time-travelling. Sliding fully right (or closing the Atlas) returns to the present. Data: a bounded ring of yearly per-hub [population, trade] frames (CampaignSim.year_frames, hub indices are stable so old frames simply omit later foundings) served by campaign_get_era_frame.

🔥 Per-good Trade Heat (Goods Codex)

📖 Goods Codex ▸ 🧵 silk ▾ 🔥 this good

One click beside the Codex's good picker filters the 🔥 Trade Heat overlay to a single commodity — silk heat vs grain heat look completely different. Changing the selected good moves the filter with it; closing the Codex returns heat to all goods. Data: accrue_flow now also feeds a per-hub per-good yearly ledger (hub_good_trade, flat hub×good, ~27 KB), snapshotted each New Year; campaign_get_good_heat(name) serves the points. The same ledger gives every trade basin its top goods — Regions cards now read "silk · wine" beside the town count.

💾 Compact saves (invisible, but you'll feel it)

WhatBeforeAfter
Campaign blob (campaign_sim row, .campaign files) Plain JSON — multi-MB on a mature campaign, rewritten at every autosave Z1: + base64(zstd(json)) — typically 5–10× smaller. JSON stays the format underneath, so the append-serde(default) save-compat strategy is untouched; legacy plain rows load via the prefix check. Round-trip + legacy passthrough covered by a unit test.
