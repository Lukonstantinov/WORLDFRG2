Human · Settlement · Economy · Goods — 18 Proposals (Part 2)

WorldForge 2 · continuation brainstorm · ranked by feasibility within each domain · ⭐ = standout value

Companion to Part 1 (climate & biology, ideas 1–20) in climate-correlation-brainstorm.html.

Why most of these are cheap: the campaign side is the engine's deepest layer — the tick sim (sim/tick.rs) already runs houses, banks, coin, wars and markets; there are 45 goods with recipe DAGs, routed trade trunks, and a political/centrality ranking. Most ideas below are reuse + a new panel/overlay.
Human

Settlement & people

21 City rank-size (Zipf) check

Plot settlement size/wealth vs Zipf's law; flag missing primate city or too-flat hierarchy.

CritiqueA lens, not a verdict — worlds may differ.
Feasibility: high

22 "Why is this city here?" dossier ⭐

Break down the habitability score + tag the site archetype (ford, harbor, oasis, hill).

ValueExplainable placement lore; pairs with climate dossier #6.
Feasibility: high

23 Travel-time / itinerary calc ⭐

Two settlements → journey time by foot/horse/cart/ship/river over the coarse cost grid.

ValueTop GM tool.
CritiqueNeeds per-mode speed constants.
Feasibility: high

24 Settlement tiers + real analogs

hamlet→metropolis population bands + a real historical analog city.

CritiqueCurated mapping.
Feasibility: high

25 Population & carrying capacity

Plausible populations from fertility/area; density overlay.

CritiqueTech-level assumption; rough.
Feasibility: high–medium

26 Geographic toponym generator

Name rivers/mountains/regions via names.rs + cultures, with meanings.

CritiqueLinguistic rabbit hole.
Feasibility: high

27 Persistent roads & waystations

Promote trade trunks into named roads with inns spaced a day apart.

CritiquePersistence + placement is new work.
Feasibility: medium

28 Defensibility / fortification

Terrain + chokepoints + rivers → where castles/walls make sense.

CritiqueHeuristic-heavy.
Feasibility: medium
Economy

Markets, wealth & shocks

29 Wealth inequality (Gini) & mobility ⭐

Chart Gini, concentration, house turnover from the tick sim; optional historical compare.

ValueMakes the living economy legible; on-theme.
Feasibility: high

30 City price index / cost-of-living

Basket-of-goods CPI per hub to compare cities ("Y is 2.3× pricier").

ValueConcrete cross-city comparison.
Feasibility: high

31 Trade balance & comparative advantage

Per-region net exporter/importer + Ricardian specialization highlight.

ValueReveals regional economic identity.
Feasibility: high

32 Economic shock timeline

Replay crashes/wars/bank failures with cause→effect chains.

CritiqueMostly UI over existing logs/why-chains.
Feasibility: medium (UI)

33 Historical economy-era analog

Tag the economy (barter/coinage/banking) to a real era.

CritiqueInterpretive, fuzzy.
Feasibility: medium

34 Wages & labor layer

Wages by city from labor demand vs cost of living.

CritiqueNew variable to balance.
Feasibility: medium
Goods

Commodities & chains

35 Goods provenance / chain tracer ⭐

Click a finished good → trace back through recipe DAG + actual routes/belts.

ValueSpectacular; reuses DAG + GoodFlowPanel.
Feasibility: high

36 Real-world commodity history cards

Each good gets a short real-history card (silk road, spice trade, salt-as-money).

ValueCheap lore depth; on-theme.
Feasibility: very high

37 Scarcity ↔ luxury heatmap

Overlay cheap-commodity vs prized-luxury from market price gradients.

ValueShows where a good is worth carrying.
Feasibility: high

38 Seasonal harvest calendar

Perishables/harvests vary by season per region (shares season model with #12/#19).

CritiqueNeeds a season model.
Feasibility: medium
