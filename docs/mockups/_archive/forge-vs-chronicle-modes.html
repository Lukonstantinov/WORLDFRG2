WorldForge 2 → two subproducts, one app

Design report for the Forge / Chronicle split and the campaign economy re-derivation from geography. Colours & values mirror the app (uiStore.appMode, campaign_start_sim, Toolbar.tsx).

The seam in one line: the finalized world hands the campaign only the physical substrate — city sites, founding population, productive potential, the goods spec, and the trade-route network. The campaign re-derives prices, wealth and trade-flow volumes live, starting at founding. Routes in, flows out.

2 · Routes in, flows out — the same network, two different truths

Worldgen — potential

Every lane drawn at a uniform, solver-derived width — a static snapshot of where trade could flow. Good as a map of the world's trade geography.

Campaign — live (Year 12)

Same route network — but lane width is the live yearly volume (flow_accum → flow_year). Veyra's houses have cornered the trade; the Kol–Mira lane sits quiet this year (dashed).

3 · Founding prices evolve live (no seeded worldgen numbers)

Before: hub prices were copied from the static market solve. After: every hub starts at each good's intrinsic base_value (autarky) and the tick's stock-driven relax discovers real, trade-driven prices over the first weeks — start at founding, evolve live.

Illustrative values. The point: prices are no longer a frozen echo of the worldgen preview — they emerge from the campaign's own production, freight and demand, and diverge across cities as trade develops.

What crosses the boundary (unchanged): city sites, founding population, per-hub productive potential (base_per_capita), goods spec, and the route / reachability graph — plus a founding food-viability constraint (a city wouldn't exist where it can't feed itself), which keeps the live-evolving start from famine-crashing. What is re-derived: prices, wealth, trade-flow volumes, and which routes are active — all emergent.
