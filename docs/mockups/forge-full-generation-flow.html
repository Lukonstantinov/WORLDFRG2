Forge owns the whole generation pipeline

Phase 3: everything generated — geography and settlements, trade goods, political & economy — is completed in Forge. A single Finalize after Economy locks the world and hands off to Chronicle, which now owns only the living campaign tick.

Why: steps 7–10 never actually needed a frozen world — sim_biological and sim_generate_settlements write derived tile columns (no ensure_unfrozen), and political/economy are query-only. The old "freeze after step 6, then do 7–10" was a UI gate, not a technical one. So this is a pure frontend workflow reorganization — no backend change.
1–6 Geography→ 7–10 Settlements · Trade · Political · Economy→ 🔒 Finalize & Play→ 📜 Chronicle (campaign tick)
