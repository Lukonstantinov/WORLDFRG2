Two visual changes

(1) Manufactured goods no longer get a map overlay, and (2) a city a house is developing now shows a trade-base badge.

1 · Manufactured goods — no overlay (they're made in cities, not grown)

Cloth, metalware, refined sugar, citrus liqueur have no natural belt. The backend (compute_good_regions) used to synthesize a misleading "supply-zone" belt from their input goods; it now emits nothing for them, and the Trade-Goods toggle list (Toolbar.tsx) hides their toggles.

2 · Trade-base badge in the city inspector

When a merchant house develops an under-traded small city as a base of operations, the HubPanel header shows it. Driven by the new patron field on HubDetail.
