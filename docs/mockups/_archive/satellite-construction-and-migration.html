Satellite Construction & Route-Bound Migration

Design mockups (not built yet). A metropolis council spends city wealth to found & build a satellite over ~10 years: buying caravans/ships, hauling food, preservables and construction goods along real trade routes, passing 5 build stages, and weathering events that speed or delay the works. When finished, the construction window is replaced by a normal city window — but the satellite is bound to its metropolis (all trade routes through the mother city first). Below: 3 window variants, the migration rework bound to trade routes (3 variants), and open questions.

B · Route-Bound Migration — three visual variants

Today migration draws a smooth arc between two cities and does not follow the trade network — so people appear to "jump" across terrain. The rework routes every migrant flow strictly along existing trade routes (multi-hop through intermediate cities). Below the same 3-city corridor drawn 3 ways. Cultures: ■ Latin ■ Hellene ■ Phoenician.

1 · Flowing dots on the route

Dots ride the polyline; colour = culture; spacing = volume. Cheapest to render.

2 · Culture ribbon (width = volume)

Overlapping ribbons per culture, thickness ∝ people/yr. Reads "who flows where" best.

Selected city shows inbound origins + cultures; map isolates only the trade routes those migrants used.
Core migration rule (all variants): a migrant flow is only possible if an unbroken chain of trade routes connects origin → destination; the drawn path is the actual routed polyline (coarse-grid path, same geometry as trade trunks), not a straight arc. No route ⇒ no migration. Volume is capped by the route's capacity (piracy/blockade throttle it).
