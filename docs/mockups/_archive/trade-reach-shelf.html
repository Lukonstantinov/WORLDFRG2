Trade reach — continental shelf is “reachable sea”

User rule: “If a tile is water but it is shelf, then it means there is a reachable sea (not high sea).” Coastal navigation hugged shallow shelf water; only crossing the deep / high sea (beyond the shelf) was the hard, reach-limited part. The coarse trade-cost grid now treats shelf water as cheap, unrestricted coastal sea and counts only non-shelf deep ocean against the open-water crossing limit.

Before — only the 1-cell coastal ring was “reachable”

Sea was “coastal” (cheap, reachable) only within ONE cell of land. Anything past that ring read as open ocean, so a coastal hop along a wide shelf bay counted as a crossing — small/distant cities got dropped (the disconnected dynamic-flow gaps), and routes detoured overland or beelined straight at the city.

After — the whole shelf is reachable coastal sea

Shelf water is cheap shipping and is excluded from the crossing limit, so A↔B hug the shelf and even C links via a short deep-sea hop at the narrows. Only the genuinely open ocean still counts toward max_crossing — small & coastal cities stop being dropped.
