WorldForge 2 — Colony Supply & Lifecycle  refined design · proposals only

A settlement colony can't feed itself at first, so the founding metropolis must keep it supplied (contracts or shipments, fully subsidised) until it grows self-sufficient. Same-continent only; a same-continent bank funds it and becomes its bank + mint; the metropolis holds a trade monopoly until independence.

The backing bank's family becomes the colony's main bank & mint — it sets the colony's council, mints its coin, and seeds coin-trust.
Why this makes cities & banks think twice
  • A full year of food held in reserve = locked capital.
  • Ongoing subsidy: the metropolis pays the food bill every shipment until self-sufficiency.
  • The bank stakes a real loan — if the colony collapses, the loan defaults and can feed a crash.
  • Reward: a trade monopoly on the colony + dividends, and eventually a daughter city.
