WorldForge 2 — Colonisation & the holes in the economy  brainstorm · proposals only

Where the economy has holes (grounded in tick.rs)

1 · "Colonies" are just estates. maybe_colonize spawns another production satellite via create_estate — it never becomes a real settlement that grows population, hosts houses, or graduates into a market city.
2 · Banks can't finance ventures. Banks lend to houses (trade credit) and polis treasuries (war/works) — but there is no productive lending: they can't fund colonies, new estates, or fleets. Capital sits idle.
3 · No labour migration. Population grows/declines in place; workers never move from a busted city to a booming one, so booms don't pull labour and busts don't shed it.
4 · No infrastructure. Freight cost per good is static — nobody builds roads / ports / canals to cut it, so geography never improves.
5 · No risk pooling. Storm / piracy / shipworm losses fall entirely on one house; there's no marine insurance market (a natural bank/guild product).
6 · No joint-stock companies. A house acts alone, capped by personal wealth — there's no vehicle to pool many backers' capital for a big voyage or colony (the VOC / East India model).
7 · Thin top-end wealth sinks. Beyond estates/fleets/banks, great fortunes have nowhere to go — no palaces, patronage, dowries, or conspicuous consumption recycling money down.

15+ new features / fixes

Colonisation & expansion
  • Colonial Venture + joint-stock backing (above).
  • Colony graduation Outpost→Colony→Town→City.
  • Chartered companies — a standing multi-backer firm that runs several colonies/lanes and pays dividends.
  • Charter monopolies contestable by rivals (war hook).
Credit & finance
  • Productive bank lending — banks fund colonies/estates/fleets, not just war.
  • Marine insurance market — pool storm/piracy/shipworm losses; underwriters profit or blow up.
  • Civic bonds — cities issue debt to citizens/houses for works & war.
  • Credit cycle — over-lending inflates a boom that the crash system pops.
Population & production
  • Labour migration — workers move from busted to booming cities (and to colonies).
  • Infrastructure — roads/ports/canals built by cities/houses cut freight on a corridor.
  • Wage/labour market — labour has a cost; skilled vs unskilled; guild apprenticeships.
  • Technology diffusion — techniques spread along trade routes, not just espionage.
  • Boom towns — a new deposit/good can spawn a rush settlement.
Wealth sinks & society
  • Conspicuous consumption — palaces, patronage, dowries recycle great fortunes.
  • Dynastic marriages — houses ally/merge wealth (alliance graph hook).
  • Famine relief / poor laws — a city spends treasury to dampen starvation spirals.

UI / UX improvements

Navigation & legibility
